# Resetting a password or unlocking an account

Keywords: password, reset, locked, login, sign in, 2FA, two-factor

1. Go to the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". A reset link is emailed and is valid for 30 minutes.
2. After 5 failed attempts an account locks for 15 minutes. It unlocks automatically.
3. If two-factor codes are not arriving, check the device clock is set automatically; codes are time-based.
4. Support can force a reset for the account owner only, after verifying the owner's email.
