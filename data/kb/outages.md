# Outages and degraded service

Keywords: down, outage, not loading, error 500, 502, 503, timeout, sync stopped, cannot access, production

- Check the status page first. If an incident is open, link it and give the next update time.
- If no incident is open and the customer reports a full outage, treat it as P1: escalate to on-call immediately.
- Enterprise customers have a 1-hour response SLA on P1 issues.
- Never guess at root cause in a reply. Say what we know, what we are doing, and when the next update will come.
