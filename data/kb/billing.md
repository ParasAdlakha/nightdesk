# Billing, invoices and refunds

Keywords: invoice, billing, charge, charged twice, refund, payment, card, receipt, VAT

- Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are available under Settings > Billing.
- Duplicate charges are refunded in full within 5 to 10 business days once confirmed. Support must confirm both charge IDs before promising a refund.
- Refunds for unused months are pro-rated on annual plans only.
- VAT IDs can be added to future invoices; past invoices cannot be reissued.
