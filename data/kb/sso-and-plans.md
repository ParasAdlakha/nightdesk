# SSO, plans and upgrades

Keywords: SSO, SAML, Okta, Azure AD, upgrade, plan, seats, pricing, enterprise, cancel, cancellation

- SAML SSO (Okta, Azure AD, Google) is included on Business and Enterprise plans.
- Seats can be added at any time from Settings > Team; billing is pro-rated.
- Cancellation requests are handled by the account manager, never by an automated reply. Flag the account and draft a holding reply.
- Upgrade questions from Business customers go to sales as a lead.
